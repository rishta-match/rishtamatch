Privacy Policy

This is a starter template for the demo version. Before launching to real users, have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer and update it to match how the final app actually works.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address and the profile details you choose to add: name, gender, age, city, education, profession, community, religion, height, diet, about-me and an optional profile photo.

Who can see it

Your profile details and photo are visible to other logged-in members so they can find and contact you. Your email address and password are never shown to other members. Interests you send, your shortlist and blocked profiles are visible only to you (and the member you sent an interest to).

Where it is stored

Account and profile data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, with access rules that stop members from editing other members' data.

Sample profiles

Profiles marked "Sample" are fictional and created for demonstration. They are not real people.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time, block or report other members, and ask us to delete your account and data by contacting the site owner.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your language, theme and sample-profile shortlist, and to keep you logged in. Clearing your browser data removes this.

Contact

For questions or to request removal of data, contact the site owner through the RishtaMatch GitHub repository.
