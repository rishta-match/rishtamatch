Privacy Policy

This is a starter template for the demo version. Before launching to real users, have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer and update it to match how the final app actually works.

What we collect

In the current demo, the profile you create (name, age, city, education, profession, community, religion, height, diet, about-me and photo) is saved only in your own browser on your device. It is not sent to any server.

Sample profiles

The profiles shown on the site are fictional samples for demonstration. They are not real people.

When real accounts are added

We will collect only what is needed to run the service, store it securely, never sell your personal data, and let you view, edit or delete your profile. We will ask for your consent before showing your photo or contact details to others.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your profile, shortlist, language and theme. Clearing your browser data removes this.

Contact

For questions or to request removal of data, contact the site owner through the RishtaMatch GitHub repository.
